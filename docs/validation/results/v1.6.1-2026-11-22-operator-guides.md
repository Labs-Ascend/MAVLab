## Observations

1. In Control, selected **Phone sensors**, saved neutral, and armed. The screen showed **Input accepted** and **Takeoff · 10 m**. With the phone near neutral, local takeoff engaged Alt Hold. The airborne Cockpit displayed 10.1 m above ground, and SIM rendered the quadcopter in flight. [First flight on Android](../../user/first-flight.md) · [Navigate MAVLab](../../user/navigation-and-display.md).
2. Used **Land** in Control. Cockpit subsequently showed 0.0 m and **DISARMED** after automatic disarm. Ops showed recording stopped. [Landing screenshot](../../user/images/v1.6.1/cockpit-disarmed.webp).
3. Opened QGC on Android with MAVLab disarmed. QGC Fly displayed the simulated vehicle, GPS, and battery. On returning to MAVLab, Ops showed a validated GCS heartbeat. After closing Android QGC, Ops returned to disconnected. [Same-device evidence](../../user/connect-qgroundcontrol.md#same-android-device) · [Disconnect screenshot](../../user/images/v1.6.1/ops-disconnected.webp).
4. With Android QGC closed, desktop QGC discovered MAVLab over Wi-Fi with default UDP auto-connect. QGC displayed live flight mode, GPS, and battery. Ops again showed a validated heartbeat. [Desktop evidence](../../user/connect-qgroundcontrol.md#desktop-over-wi-fi).

The pictures show the released app UI. The Android captures omit system bars where present. The QGC maps show the simulated home point in Nairobi. Mission upload or execution, GCS command delivery, injected failures, recording and export, and Android versions other than the test phone's Android 12 were outside this capture run. The local flight used no QGC session. Wi-Fi was available on the test phone. This change updates documentation only.
